Privacy notice

This notice explains what information may be handled when you visit NextTalentGlobal’s website or contact the consultancy about recruitment and hiring services.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Who is responsible

NextTalentGlobal is the name used for this recruitment consultancy. For privacy questions or requests, email nextalentglobal.hr@gmail.com or call +91 99152 61554.

Information we handle

  • Messages you send: If you contact us by email or phone, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, contact details and hiring enquiry.
  • Website and device information: Our hosting and content providers may process technical request data, such as IP address, browser/device information, requested pages and security logs, to deliver and protect the site.
  • Recruitment information: If you later share candidate or employee information with us as part of a recruitment assignment, we will handle it for that assignment and provide further information where appropriate.

The enquiry form currently shown on this site is a demonstration only. It does not transmit or store the details entered. Please use the published email or phone details to contact us. Do not submit sensitive personal information or candidate documents through this demonstration form.

How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, discuss and deliver recruitment services, maintain business communications, protect the website, and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not currently use this website for advertising profiles or analytics tracking.

Service providers and international processing

The website uses Vercel for hosting if deployed there, Google Fonts for typography, and jsDelivr to deliver the GSAP animation library. When your browser requests these services, those providers may receive technical connection information under their own terms and privacy practices. We do not control their processing. Review their current privacy information before launch and confirm the hosting and service providers actually used by your deployment.

Information may be processed in countries where the relevant provider operates. Where applicable law requires safeguards for an international transfer, the responsible business should put those safeguards in place.

Retention and security

We keep business correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a relationship or recruitment assignment, and meet legal or dispute-resolution requirements. Hosting and security logs are retained according to the hosting provider’s settings. The operating business should establish and follow a documented retention schedule for candidate records.

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live and which laws apply, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of personal information, to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or to raise a complaint with a relevant authority. To make a request, contact us using the details above. We may need to verify your identity and will respond as required by applicable law.

India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the 2025 Rules have staged commencement dates. This notice is intended to describe current site practices, not to assert that every provision is already in force; the business should review its obligations as each provision takes effect. See the official commencement notification and official Rules notification.

Cookies and browser storage

This site uses one strictly functional, first-party cookie named ntg_cookie_notice to remember that the cookie notice was acknowledged. It stores no name, contact details or browsing history and expires after six months. The site does not currently set analytics or advertising cookies. You can clear this cookie in your browser; the notice will then appear again. The site also uses local browser storage temporarily to complete smooth navigation between pages.

Third-party font and animation files are requested from Google Fonts and jsDelivr. These requests may expose technical connection data to those providers, as described above. The site does not currently load third-party analytics or advertising tools.

Children and updates

This business website is intended for employers and working professionals, not children. We may update this notice when the site or its data practices change. The current version will be published on this page with its revision date.
